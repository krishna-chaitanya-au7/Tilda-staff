# Google Play Advertising ID Declaration

## Error Message

"Explanation for 'advertising ID' incomplete"
"All developers targeting apps to Android 13 or higher must inform us if their app uses an advertising ID."

## What This Means

Google Play requires you to declare whether your app uses the Android Advertising ID. This is a policy requirement for apps targeting Android 13+.

## How to Fix

### Step 1: Click "Fill out the declaration form"

In the modal dialog, click the blue link **"Fill out the declaration form"**.

### Step 2: Answer the Declaration

You'll be asked: **"Does your app use an advertising ID?"**

**For Tilda Staff app, the answer is likely: NO**

Since Tilda Staff is a messaging/communication app for staff members, it typically doesn't use advertising IDs unless you're:
- Showing ads in the app
- Using analytics that track users for advertising
- Using third-party SDKs that collect advertising IDs

### Step 3: Select "No"

1. **Select**: "No, my app does not use an advertising ID"
2. **Click "Save"** or "Submit"

### If Your App DOES Use Advertising ID

If you're using any advertising SDKs or analytics that collect Ad IDs, you would:
1. Select "Yes"
2. Provide details about how it's used
3. Explain the purpose

## For Tilda Staff

**Recommended answer: NO**

Tilda Staff appears to be a staff communication/messaging app, which typically doesn't need advertising IDs. Unless you're specifically using ad networks or advertising analytics, select "No".

## After Declaration

1. The error should disappear
2. You can proceed with your app submission
3. The declaration will be saved for future releases

## Note

This is a one-time declaration (or needs to be updated if your app's behavior changes). Once you declare it, you won't need to do it again unless you add advertising functionality later.
